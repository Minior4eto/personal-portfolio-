Distro shelf

Part-time distro hopper. Here’s what I’ve tried.

  • Arch independent · rolling Build it yourself from the ground up.
  • Artix Arch-based · no systemd Arch without systemd, with other init systems.
  • Omarchy Arch-based An opinionated, ready-to-go Arch setup.
  • GhostBSD FreeBSD-based A desktop-friendly take on the BSD side.
  • Fedora RPM · Red Hat family Recent software with sensible defaults.
  • Debian apt · stable The steady base lots of other distros build on.
